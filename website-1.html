Questions we are often asked

How do I place an order?

Fill in the request form or message us on WhatsApp. We give you a reference number, confirm the price and delivery, and you approve before anything is bought or shipped.

Which towns do you deliver to?

We start in Limbe, Buea and Douala, with more cities to come. Message us your location and we will confirm.

How are marketplace sellers checked?

Our team registers each seller, checks their identity and shop, and only then lists them. Private details are never shown on the website.

Can you help me sell my products abroad?

Yes. Tell us the product, the quantity and where you want to sell. We look for buyers and arrange the shipping and documents.

How do I pay safely?

We confirm the amount and payment method with you through our official WhatsApp number before you pay. Do not pay anyone who has not been confirmed by KMDS.
