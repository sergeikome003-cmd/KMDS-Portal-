About KMDS

Built in Limbe. Building global trade.

Kingmakers Delivery Service (KMDS) connects Cameroonian businesses and families to the world: procurement and sourcing from suppliers abroad, freight and forwarding, customs support, and trusted last-mile delivery to your door.

Last-mile delivery in Cameroon is our starting point. From here we are growing step by step into a full global trade and logistics company, without heavy assets, using trusted partners for air, sea and road.

A word from KMDS

KMDS started with one simple promise: if you trust us with your parcel or your order, we treat it like our own. We began in Limbe, moving parcels between our neighbouring cities. Today we are building a company that brings products from the world to Cameroon, helps Cameroonian sellers reach more customers, and connects businesses across borders. Our vision is big, and we are growing it step by step, with honest prices and clear communication. Thank you for trusting us.
