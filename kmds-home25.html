About KMDS

Our Story and Our Vision

Kingmakers Delivery Service (KMDS) connects Cameroonian businesses and families to the world: procurement and sourcing from suppliers abroad, freight and forwarding, customs support, and trusted last-mile delivery to your door.

Last-mile delivery is where we start, and where trust is earned. From here we are growing step by step into a full global trade and logistics company, without heavy assets, using trusted partners for air, sea and road.

KMDS started with one simple promise: if you trust us with your parcel or your order, we treat it like our own. Our vision is big, and we are growing it step by step, with honest prices and clear communication. Thank you for trusting us.
